Why this app exists

ETH Mensa by Alexandre Reol is the nicest way to check lunch at ETH and UZH, and it's open source. It runs on iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch, but not on Android.

Mensa Zürich brings it to Android as a native app, built with the same care for the small things: menus that load fast, a Legi card one tap away, and last week's habits turning into this week's sorting.

It has no account, no ads and no analytics. Your settings stay on your phone, and the app only talks to the mensas' own servers. It's free and released under the GPLv3, like the app it's based on.

If something is wrong or missing, tell me.
