Privacy policy

Mensa Zürich doesn't collect your data. This page explains what the app and this website do with the little they handle, and which servers they talk to.

Effective 2 November 2026

No account

You never sign up. If you open the Legi card, you sign in on ETH's own login page.

No tracking

No ads, no analytics, no crash reporting, no tracking of any kind, in the app or on this website.

Stays on your phone

Your settings, favorites and location stay on your device. The developer receives nothing.

Who is responsible

Mensa Zürich is an independent, unofficial app made by mrghxst. It isn't affiliated with ETH Zürich, the University of Zurich or ZFV. This policy covers the Mensa Zürich app for Android, its Wear OS app and this website.

What the developer receives

Nothing. Mensa Zürich has no server of its own and no account system. It contains no advertising, analytics, crash reporting or tracking libraries. The developer can't see who uses the app, how, or where.

If you install the app from Google Play, Google handles the download and may show the developer anonymous, aggregated statistics such as the number of installs. That is covered by Google's privacy policy.

What stays on your device

The app stores these things on your phone or watch, and nowhere else:

  • Your settings: language, theme, the price group to show, your allergens, filters and reminders.
  • Your favorite and hidden mensas, and how often you open each mensa, which is used for smart sorting.
  • Downloaded menus, photos and map tiles, cached so the app is fast and works offline.
  • Your location, only if you allow it (see below).
  • The login session of ETH's Legi card page, only if you sign in there (see below).

If Android's device backup is on, Android may include the app's settings in your backup, under your Google account and Google's terms. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes everything above.

Location

Location access is optional and off until you allow it. With it, the app can:

  • sort mensas by distance while you use the app, and
  • if you turn on campus-based reminders, ask Android to tell it when you arrive at or leave a campus (geofencing), so it can remind you of the menu only when you're there. This uses location in the background, which Android asks you to allow separately.

Your location is processed on your device and is never sent anywhere by the app. You can revoke access at any time in Android's settings, and turn off campus reminders in the app.

Legi card

The Legi card is ETH Zürich's digital student and staff card. When you open it, the app shows ETH's own page (eduapp.ethz.ch) in a built-in browser, and you sign in there with your ETH account. Your username and password go directly to ETH. The app doesn't read or store them.

Like any browser, the built-in browser keeps the cookies ETH sets so you stay signed in. They're stored only in the app's storage on your device. ETH's privacy policy applies to the Legi card and the login. Clearing the app's storage signs you out.

Servers the app contacts

To show menus and the Legi card, the app connects to these services directly. Each request reveals the usual technical data, such as your IP address and the app's name and version, to that service, which handles it under its own privacy policy.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub receives your IP address and browser details to deliver the pages and may keep them in its server logs, as described in GitHub's privacy statement. The website itself sets no cookies and uses no analytics. Its fonts are hosted here too, so no font service is contacted.

The “Today's lunch” section loads the current menu and dish photos directly from ETH Zürich's server (idapps.ethz.ch) in your browser, which shows ETH your IP address. If you switch the website to light mode, that choice is saved in your browser's local storage and never leaves your browser.

Your rights

Under the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection, and the GDPR where it applies, you can ask what personal data is held about you and have it corrected or deleted. The developer holds no personal data about you, so there is nothing to hand over or delete. Everything the app stores is on your own device, where you can delete it by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it.

For data held by ETH Zürich, ZFV, GitHub, OpenFreeMap or Google, contact them directly.

Children

The app is suitable for everyone and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If the app or this website starts handling data differently, this page will be updated before the change is released, with a new effective date at the top.
