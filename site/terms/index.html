Menus, prices and allergens

Menus, prices, opening hours, photos, nutrition and allergen information come from ETH Zürich and ZFV and are shown as published by them. The app may simplify how they're displayed. They can be incomplete, out of date or wrong, and dishes and recipes can change at short notice.

If you have an allergy or intolerance, always confirm the ingredients with the mensa staff before eating. Allergen information in the app is a guide, not a guarantee. It doesn't cover traces or cross-contamination, and hiding meals with an allergen only hides dishes the mensa has labelled.

The price charged at the till is the one that counts. Opening hours can differ on holidays and during semester breaks.
